Mockup of the web app - click around. Design notes

Start page

  • All lists of all spaces. The space name is only a small label (hidden with just one space).
  • ⋮ menu: New list, Spaces & sharing, Settings.

List page

  • No checkboxes: tap an item to check it, tap again to uncheck. ⋯ opens its details.
  • Checked items stay in place; the sort button in the header bar moves them to the end on demand.
  • + opens the add dialog (stays open for the next item).

New list

  • Name plus the space as chips - preselected is the space of the last used list. With only one space the chips are hidden.
  • After "Create" the new list opens directly.

Spaces & sharing

  • One row per space with its members. + creates a space.
  • A space page shows members, open invites and the space's categories and shops. ⋮: rename and delete (owner), leave (member).
  • Owners manage members via ⋯ (make owner, remove). "Invite someone" asks how long the link is valid, then shows it with Copy and Share.

Settings

  • Plain sections: profile, display, AI agents, account. Language and theme are segmented buttons and apply at once (try the theme).
  • "Move checked items to the end" is gone - that is the sort button now.
  • API tokens on their own page; a new token is shown once with Copy.

Open questions

  • "Tap = check, ⋯ = details" - or swipe to delete?
